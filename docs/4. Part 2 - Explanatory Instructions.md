When finished, export your explanatory notebook in HTML or pdf format.

### Step 3: Review and Submit

There’s one last thing to do before you submit your project. You should closely read the **project rubric**, which your reviewer will use to evaluate your work. Perform a self-review to assess the quality of your work. If there is any rubric point on which you don’t feel confident, then go back and make improvements before submitting your project for review.

## (Optional) Get Feedback

Though not required, we suggest you get feedback from at least one person before submitting your project. By sharing your work with others, you can get input from a different perspective that catches things you may have missed initially.

Share your final HTML/PDF outputs with someone in person and provide live feedback on what they get from your analysis.

You might need to ask specific questions to prompt your reader. The following questions might be good starters; be sure to follow up or come up with your questions:

- What do you notice about each visualization?
- What questions do you have about the data?
- What relationships do you notice?
- What do you think is the main takeaway from the analysis?
- Is there anything that you don’t understand from the plots?
